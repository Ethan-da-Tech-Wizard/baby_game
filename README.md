# 👶 Baby Game: The Ultimate Chromebook Toddler Sensory Playground

A responsive, high-energy, sensory web game tailored specifically for toddlers (aged 1–4) running on Chrome OS Chromebooks (as well as Linux, Mac, and Windows).

Every single key on the keyboard does something drastically different across the entire screen!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🎹 **Every Key Mapped**: Over 70+ Chromebook keys trigger unique fullscreen visual effects, sounds, and persistent interactive layers.
- 🦈 **Baby Shark Mode**: Toggle the iconic *Baby Shark* melody on and off with key `T` or the on-screen toggle!
- 🦁 **10+ Animal Sounds**: Procedurally synthesized cow, duck, dog, cat, sheep, lion, frog, elephant, bird, dinosaur, and more!
- ⚡ **Anime Action Backdrops**: Toddler-appropriate, heroic, high-energy action loops (DBZ Super Saiyan Ki aura, Naruto Ninja Dash, Luffy Gear Charge, Pokémon Island) with **zero adult content, nudity, or gore**. Cycle anytime with `Tab`.
- 🐹 **Dancing Pokémon Troupe & Kaiju Crew**:
  - **Press `P`**: Cycles dance squads and triggers the **Ultimate 10-Pokémon Dance Stampede**!
  - **Direct Key Summons**:
    - **`K`**: **Kangaskhan** (Big parent beast with baby Kangaskhan waving from pouch!)
    - **`N`**: **Nidoking** (Royal purple armored rhino-monster with drill horn!)
    - **`B`**: **Blastoise** (Water turtle blasting animated twin shoulder water cannons!)
    - **`V`**: **Venusaur** (Tropical pink flower bloom with animated whipping vine whips!)
    - **`A`**: **Aerodactyl** (Prehistoric pterosaur dragon flapping violet wings in flight!)
    - **`C`**: **Charizard** (Orange winged fire dragon flapping wings and blowing flame smoke!)
    - **`Z`**: **Zigzagoon** (Early route raccoon with zig-zag bristly fur doing a zig-zag dash dance!)
    - **`S`**: **Starly** (Gen 4 Sinnoh early bird with fluttering wings and curled black head crest!)
    - **`J`**: **Togepi** (Eggshell baby with red & blue triangles waving its tiny arms!)
    - **`P`**: **Pikachu & Party** (Electric mouse wiggling ears with red glowing cheeks!)
- 🌈 **Crazy Visual FX**: Rainbow warp, fireworks, lightning storms, raining clouds, bubble popping, bouncing physics balls, laser cannons, disco strobe, and confetti cannons!
- 🔒 **Toddler-Proof Chromebook Defense**:
  - Intercepts keys like `Backspace`, `Tab`, `Alt`, and `Space` to prevent accidental navigation or tab closure.
  - One-click Fullscreen toggle (`F4` or on-screen button) so tiny hands can't accidentally close the window.
  - Multi-touch screen support for touch Chromebooks!
- 🚀 **100% Offline & Zero Dependency**: Built using standard HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and SpeechSynthesis. No missing external links, works completely offline in the car or airplane!

## 🚀 How to Play on your Chromebook

### Option 1: One-Click Web Play (GitHub Pages)
1. Open Google Chrome on the Chromebook.
2. Visit the GitHub Pages link: `https://ethan-da-tech-wizard.github.io/baby_game/`
3. Click anywhere on the screen to start audio and enter **Fullscreen Mode**.
4. Let your toddler mash away!

### Option 2: Local Play (No Internet Required)
1. Download or clone this repository to the Chromebook's "My Files" / Downloads folder.
2. Double click `index.html` to open it in Chrome.
3. Press `F4` (the Fullscreen key on the top row) and enjoy!

## 🗺️ Quick Parent Reference Guide

- **Baby Shark Toggle**: Press **`T`**
- **Dancing Pokémon Toggle**: Press **`P`**
- **Change Anime Backdrop**: Press **`Tab`**
- **Clear Active Layers / Reset**: Press **`Esc`**
- **Fullscreen**: Press **`F4`** or top-row `[ ]`
- **Numbers (1–0)**: Speaks numbers and spawns cute animals!
- **Spacebar**: Triggers a Giant Celebration Parade with bass drop!
- **Enter**: Screen Shake Earthquake + Mega Fireworks!
